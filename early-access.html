Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Bucksy and our early access program.

Bucksy is a modern expense planner that helps you track your spending, manage budgets, and build healthier money habits. It provides a clean, intuitive interface for organizing your finances and gaining insights into your spending patterns.

Bucksy will offer a freemium model. The core expense tracking and budget planning features will be available for free, with additional premium features available for paying customers. Early access members will receive exclusive founding member pricing.

Early access will begin in phases, starting with those who join our waitlist first. We'll begin inviting users from the waitlist in early 2025, with priority given to founding members. You'll receive an email invitation when it's your turn to join.

Your data security is our top priority. Bucksy uses bank-level encryption to protect your information, both in transit and at rest. We never share your financial data with third parties, and you maintain full control over your information at all times.
