What's inside

Built for the way a month actually works.

Income, fixed expenses, spending and savings live on one screen, so the month adds up without a second spreadsheet.

One month, one ledger

Salary, fixed expenses, investments and savings share a single monthly layout, with a settlement bar showing cleared versus remaining.

Paid, not guessed

Tick items off as they clear. Paid amounts and progress save to your account, so the month stays current when you come back.

Budgets that speak up

Set a monthly cap per category. The bar fills as you spend, turns amber as you approach the limit and clay when you cross it.

Numbers you can read

An allocation donut, a six-month income-versus-outflow trend, month-over-month insights and your savings rate, all from your own data.

Accounts and routing

Each account shows the transfer balance it needs and where its debits land, so payday moves are planned before the money moves.

Backup on your terms

Download a JSON copy of your planner whenever you want, and load it back later. Your numbers never depend on a single tab.

Private by design

One account. Closed door.

Bucksy is a personal tool, not a sign-up product. The live app sits behind Cloudflare Access, and every request passes bot checks, rate limits and server-side login lockouts before it reaches your data.

Cloudflare AccessA Zero Trust gate in front of the app

No bank connections, no ad trackers and no third-party analytics scripts. What you enter is what the app knows.
