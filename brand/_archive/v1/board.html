Rules

Do

  • Keep money in JetBrains Mono, tabular, always.
  • Give the deer one moment per screen — never wallpaper.
  • Use amber like saffron: sparingly, where attention belongs.
  • Make failure copy honest and helpful in the same breath.
  • Use the antler glyph below 32px.

Don't

  • Currency symbols in the antlers or the eyes.
  • Blue/purple fintech gradients — ever.
  • Confetti on save; one amber dot pulse is the celebration.
  • "Oops!", guilt framing, or stock photography.
  • A second mascot. The deer is the animal, full stop.
